# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

UIS+ is a small project built for students of the Slovak University of Technology in Bratislava.
Everyone is welcome to report a bug, suggest an improvement, translate the interface or send a patch,
regardless of background, experience or field of study.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Keep discussion focused on the extension, the pages it restyles and the problem at hand.
- Accept that maintainer time is limited and that not every request will be implemented.
- Remember that reports may be read by other students; keep them free of personal data.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, harassment or discriminatory language.
- Publishing anyone's private information, including screenshots that expose names, e-mail
  addresses, student numbers or message contents.
- Using issues or pull requests to distribute credentials, scraped academic data or anything that
  would violate the university's rules.

## Screenshots and personal data

STU UIS is a signed-in system. Before attaching a screenshot to an issue, blur or crop the user
chip, message senders and any identifying numbers. A cropped screenshot of the broken component is
always enough.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a private security advisory through the
[repository security page](https://github.com/antehor/UIS-Plus/security), or by contacting the
maintainer through [@antehor](https://github.com/antehor). Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, commits, issues and pull requests that conflict
with this document, and may block accounts for repeated or severe violations.

## Scope

This document applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and to any
space where someone represents the project.
